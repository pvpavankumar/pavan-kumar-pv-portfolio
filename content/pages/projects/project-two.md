## The challenge

Complex enterprise products require configuration, pricing, visualization, and renewal journeys that remain responsive across devices while integrating with established CPQ capabilities.

## My contribution

At Deloitte Digital, I directed UI engineering and architected a scalable headless CPQ frontend using React, SAP CPQ APIs, and Threekit 3D visualization. I provided architecture leadership, code reviews, engineering standards, and mentorship to a 15-member team.

Performance audits, bundle optimization, responsive architecture, and WCAG-aligned modernization helped turn the platform into a faster and more accessible customer experience.

## Outcomes

* **50% reduction in application latency**
* **30% faster page-load time**
* **25% increase in mobile traffic**
* Improved consistency through reusable frontend patterns and automated testing

## Technology and practices

React, JavaScript ES6+, SAP CPQ APIs, Threekit 3D, headless architecture, responsive design, WCAG accessibility, Jest, CodeceptJS, Lighthouse, CI/CD, and Agile delivery.
