## The challenge

Enterprise customers need a clear journey from commitment through onboarding, purchase assistance, consumption, and ongoing coverage. Internal stakeholders also need a consistent view of customer engagement.

## My contribution

As a Staff Software Engineer at ServiceNow, I led a 10-member Digital Technology engineering team delivering high-availability applications for the enterprise Commit-to-Consume program. I architected customer onboarding, assisted-checkout, and real-time consumption and coverage dashboards while partnering with product and enterprise architecture leaders.

I also helped integrate AI-driven automation across the customer lifecycle to reduce operational friction and manual oversight, with a focus on security, reliability, and responsiveness.

## Focus areas

* ServiceNow platform engineering and UI Builder
* High-availability enterprise applications

* Real-time consumption and coverage dashboards
