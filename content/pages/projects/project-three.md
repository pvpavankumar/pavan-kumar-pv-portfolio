## The challenge

Complex CPQ processes can require significant manual presales effort to assemble accurate quote inputs, coordinate context, and move work through multiple steps.

## My contribution

I engineered GenAI quote-creation accelerators that applied machine-learning models and prompt-driven workflows to repetitive parts of the process. The solution was designed to augment specialists rather than remove the human judgment needed for complex enterprise quoting.

Alongside this work, I used Cursor, Claude, and Windsurf to prototype workflow automations, refactor code, expand test coverage, and accelerate defect resolution.

## Outcome

**50% reduction in manual presales effort** across complex quote-creation workflows.

I also built a custom Figma plugin and design-token pipeline that streamlined designer-to-developer handoffs and accelerated design-to-code delivery.
